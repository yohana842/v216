abstract: Schnapsen is a card game that consists of two phases: The initial phase is characterized by imperfect information, while the second phase is marked by perfect information. This paper focuses on designing an optimal strategy in the first phase, with Alpha-Beta pruning being employed in the second phase. Various methods will be evaluated during the initial phase, including random rollouts, heuristics and machine learning. These methods will be implemented into “bots” and will be assessed based on their win ratio and calculation time. This research contributes to a better understanding of strategies in games with an imperfect information and provides guidelines for combining strategies with Alpha-Beta pruning.
